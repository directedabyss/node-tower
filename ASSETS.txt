New generic plates, Nov 13 2026
--------------------------------
These three plates are original generic art. No third-party art and
no earlier private mockup art is included in them.

public/plates/tower.png
  Size: 720 x 1280, RGB PNG
  How made: Original generic drawing written with a Python/PIL script
  at the exact pixel size and layout used by the scene renderer
  (floor bands, conference tables, penthouse monitor, elevator shaft
  on the right). A GenerateImage polish pass was run from that
  drawing for review. The shipped file is the exact-size PNG so
  edges stay clean under nearest-neighbor canvas scaling.
  Palette: neutral blue, white, and graphite. Modern office tower.
  No people, no text, no logos, and no gold.

public/plates/f2-floor.png
  Size: 1280 x 720, RGB PNG
  How made: Same process as tower.png. Open-plan office floor. The
  outer wall shape follows the walkable footprint used by the
  renderer so agent foot points stay on the floor. Desks sit at the
  seat coordinates. A runner and planters mark the walk path.

public/plates/f2-room.png
  Size: 864 x 1152, RGB PNG
  How made: Same process as tower.png. Office interior with a city
  window, desks at the seat coordinates, and a planter strip along
  the walk path.
